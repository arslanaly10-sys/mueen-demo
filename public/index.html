Questions

What people ask before they give an agent any authority.

Read the agent’s rules

Is this an official government service?

No. It is an independent concept for discussion, not connected to any government system. Every record in it is synthetic.

Does the agent see my password or codes?

No. The agent gets a time-limited mandate, never your password, one-time codes or session.

Does this replace Absher or Tawakkalna?

No. It is a coordination layer over existing services. Identity, eligibility, fees, the decision and the official record stay with the agency that owns each service.

Can the agent pay without asking me?

No. The details and the payment are two separate approvals, each showing the exact amount and who receives it.

Can I take the agent’s authority back?

Yes, at any time. The next step under that mandate is blocked.

Which languages does the agent speak?

Arabic and English, by text or by voice in Chrome and Edge.
